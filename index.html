UMass Robotics Club Battlebot

A 3D-printed antweight combat robot built with Team 14. The entire outer shell is the weapon: a brushless motor drives a pinion into gear teeth printed around its inner wall, spinning the shell around a two‑wheel drive base. An ESP32 runs the C++ firmware, taking Xbox controller input over Bluetooth through the Bluepad32 library and driving three PWM channels with ramped acceleration, a firmware speed cap on the weapon, a latching emergency stop, and an automatic failsafe if the controller drops.

CoForm: AI-Agent Generative CAD

A decentralized multi-agent AI design platform that transforms plain-language prompts into verified, print-ready 3D parametric CAD models. Built in 36 hours at the MIT Media Lab ScienceClaw Hackathon, the system utilizes three specialized AI agents (Mechanics, Fabrication, Measurement) powered by the NVIDIA Nemotron API to negotiate design logic, propose optimizations, and vote on printability without a central planner. The backend builds the geometry, verifies fit against reference bodies, runs 3D finite-element physics simulations (FEA) using Gmsh and CalculiX, and integrates with the Formlabs API to orient, support, and check the part for 3D printing. The agents successfully engineered and optimized custom functional parts, reducing material weight by up to 48% while maintaining safety limits.
